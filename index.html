Frequently Asked Questions

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

It's free. The catch is the deal itself: your review gets posted on my Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, so other men can learn from it too.

Will people see my face?

Only if you want them to. You choose on the form: show your face or blur it.

What do I need to send?

Screenshots of your full Hinge profile, top to bottom: every photo and every prompt. Usually 4 to 8 screenshots. Open Hinge, go to your profile, tap View, and screenshot as you scroll.

Will every profile get reviewed?

I review as many as I can, in the order they come in. If I can't get to yours, I'll email you and tell you.

How long until my review is live?

It depends on how many profiles are in the queue. I'll email you the link the day it goes up.

What happens to my screenshots?

They're stored privately and only used for your review. If you want them deleted, reply to any email from me and I'll delete them.

Will you roast me?

No. Honest, not cruel. I'll tell you what's costing you likes, but the point is to fix your profile, not to make fun of you.

I'm not on Hinge. Can I still send my profile?

Yes. Hinge is what I review most, but Bumble and Tinder profiles work too. The rules for photos are the same on every app.

Is this pickup artist stuff?

No. No lines, no tricks, no fake lifestyle. The goal is a profile that shows who you actually are, as well as possible. Nothing here would embarrass you if a woman saw it.

I'm over 40, short, balding, or not making much money. Is it worth sending?

Yes. Most of what holds a profile back has nothing to do with what you were born with. How you present what you've got matters far more than most men think.

I'm not ready to send mine yet.

No problem. Follow me on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube and learn from other men's reviews first.
