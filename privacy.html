Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Sovereign Dan collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you send your profile for a free review we collect what you enter:

  • Your first name, email address and age, and optionally your job and any notes you add.
  • The screenshots of your dating profile you upload, which can include your photos, your first name and anything written on your profile.
  • What you said is not working, whether you want your face blurred, and whether you agreed to be featured and to receive emails from us.

Like any website, our hosting provider also records technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load a page.

How we use it

  • To review your profile and publish that review as a video on Sovereign Dan's Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, based on the consent you give on the form. Your face is blurred if you ask for it.
  • To email you the link when your review is live, or to tell you if we cannot review it.
  • If you tick the box, to email you tips about dating profiles and dating. Every email has a one click unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the site working and to improve it.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by replying to any email from us. We then delete your screenshots and stop using your profile in new content.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who handles it

We use trusted services to run the site. Your information is processed by:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Google Drive), where submissions and screenshots are stored privately.
  • Telegram, which sends the site owner a notification when you send your profile.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts and delivers the website.
  • Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, where review videos are published under their own privacy policies.

These providers may process data outside your country. If we add an email service, analytics or advertising tools, such as social media pixels, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your screenshots until your review is published or we decide not to review your profile, and delete them on request. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or ask for a copy. To make a request, reply to any email you have received from us and tell us what you would like, and we will respond promptly.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service is completely secure.

Who this site is for

This site is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
